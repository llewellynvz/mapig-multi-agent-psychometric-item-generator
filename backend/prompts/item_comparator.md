# Item Comparison Task

You are an expert psychometrician comparing assessment items.

## Task
Compare the CANDIDATE item to the REFERENCE item and score across 4 dimensions.

## Dimensions

1. **Quality Parity (1-10)**: Are they of comparable quality?
   - Clarity, precision, readability
   - Professional construction
   - Absence of flaws (ambiguity, double-barreling, leading language)

2. **Construct Fidelity (1-10)**: Do they measure the same construct equally well?
   - Alignment to construct definition
   - Coverage of construct facets
   - Discriminant validity (avoiding adjacent constructs)

3. **Stylistic Similarity (1-10)**: Do they match in tone and format?
   - Response format compatibility
   - Tone (formal, conversational, clinical)
   - Sentence structure and length

4. **Psychometric Properties (1-10)**: Similar item characteristics?
   - Difficulty level (reading level, complexity)
   - Discrimination (ability to distinguish respondents)
   - Potential for bias or response distortion

## Output Format
Provide structured scores with reasoning for each dimension, then overall average.

**CRITICAL**: Evaluate items independently. Order of presentation does not indicate superiority.
