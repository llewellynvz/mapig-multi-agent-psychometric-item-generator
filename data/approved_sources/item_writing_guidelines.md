# Item writing guidelines (general)

Good self-report item stems:
- Express a single idea.
- Avoid double negatives.
- Avoid vague quantifiers ("often", "regularly") unless you define the timeframe.
- Use plain language.
- Avoid leading wording that implies a socially desirable response.
- Avoid assumptions that may not hold across subgroups (accessibility, role differences, cultural norms).

Bias and fairness considerations:
- Avoid stereotypes and loaded examples.
- Avoid references that require specific cultural knowledge.
- Avoid items that presume resources, opportunities, or experiences not shared by all respondents.
