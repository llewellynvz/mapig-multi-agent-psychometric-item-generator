# Workplace belonging

Workplace belonging refers to a person's perceived acceptance, inclusion, and connection within their workplace or team.

Key facets often include:
- Feeling accepted and valued by colleagues.
- Feeling included in informal and formal interactions.
- Feeling able to express one's authentic self without negative consequences.
- Feeling connected to a broader work community.

Measurement notes:
- Items should be specific enough to reduce ambiguity.
- Avoid idioms, culturally specific metaphors, or region-specific workplace practices.
- Prefer single-idea statements rather than double-barrelled stems.

Example phrasing patterns:
- "I feel accepted by the people I work with."
- "I feel included in work-related conversations."
